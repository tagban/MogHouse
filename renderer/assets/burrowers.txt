# Models that come up out of the ground rather than simply being there.
#
# A worm does not walk into view. It heaves itself out of the earth where it
# was waiting, which is half of what makes one recognisable - and drawing it
# standing on the surface the instant the server mentions it loses that
# entirely.
#
# One model id per line, decimal, with anything after a '#' ignored. The
# numbers come from the server's own mob_pools: the modelid column is a
# twenty-byte look blob and the model is bytes two and three, little-endian.
#
# This list is short because most of the common worms in that database carry
# model 0 and take their real one from elsewhere, so they could not be read
# out of it. Rather than guess, MOGHOUSE_SPAWN_WATCH=1 prints the model of
# everything that appears - stand near a worm, watch it spawn, and add the
# number it prints. That is a ten second job and beats a list assembled by
# somebody who could not see the thing.

426    # Leujaoam Worm, Mineral Eater
2027   # Lambton Worm, Veiled Sandworm
2029   # Veiled Gigaworm
3021   # Iron Eater
